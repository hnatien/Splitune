# Competitor teardown: Fender Play

## TL;DR
- Fender Play (Fender Digital) is a video-lesson-first app for guitar, bass and ukulele: 3,000+ bite-sized lessons, 1,000+ songs, personalized paths by instrument and genre, scrolling tablature, chord drills, backing and isolated tracks, looping, tuner, and MatchMySound listening feedback.
- 14-day free trial; US App Store IAP list shows many monthly ($7.99-$19.99) and annual ($44.99-$149.99) entries without labels; the official site was blocked to our fetcher (HTTP 403).
- Fender's 2019 launch of the Songs chord app cited a roughly 90% first-year abandonment rate for guitar lessons and about 110,000 Fender Play subscribers (trade press); see failures in `market/competitor-landscape.md`.
- 4.8/5 from 61K App Store ratings; reviews praise clear instruction but older complaints say pacing is fast and feedback is not graded.
- Splitune takeaway: brand-owned video curriculum monetizes hardware loyalty; curated songs only.

## Findings

### Core features
Music library with artists such as Nirvana, Billie Eilish, Taylor Swift, Green Day, The Rolling Stones, Metallica; structured lessons and learning paths; MatchMySound listening technology to assess playing; metronome and tempo control, scrolling tablature, chord charts and drills, backing and isolated tracks, looping, tuning; amp presets for select Fender amps; live instructor office hours, streaks, Chord Challenge; no ads ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Pick instrument and genre → follow a path of short videos → play along with scrolling tab and backing/isolated tracks → optional listening feedback. Songs are broken into riffs and parts; no user-supplied songs.

### Instruments and levels
Guitar, bass, ukulele ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939)); beginner to intermediate.

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
US App Store IAPs (unlabeled beyond Monthly/Annual): monthly $7.99, $9.99, $19.99; annual $44.99, $74.99, $89.99, $149.99; "All Access Annual Plan" $74.99; 14-day free trial ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939)). Subscription model; hardware tie-ins (amp presets). Trade press at Songs' launch reported Fender Play had about 110,000 subscribers ([Marketing Dive, Oct 2019](https://marketingdive.com/news/guitar-maker-fender-strikes-chord-with-new-songs-subscription-app/565629)).

### Platforms (official)
iOS/iPadOS 16.6+, macOS 13.5+ on Apple silicon, Apple Vision ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939)). A Google Play listing appears in search results but was not opened.

### What is public about tech and company
Seller is Fender Digital, LLC; third-party MatchMySound for audio feedback ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939)). Fender's stated strategy: raise customer lifetime value and reduce the roughly 90% first-year abandonment rate for guitar lessons ([Marketing Dive](https://marketingdive.com/news/guitar-maker-fender-strikes-chord-with-new-songs-subscription-app/565629)).

### Standout strengths
Production quality, recognizable instructors, clear lesson structure, tab view, community touches, no ads.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
Old reviews: pacing "WAY too fast," tab not staying on screen while playing along, monthly charge surprise, "no graded progression"; recent: video freezing, folders for saved content, notation requests ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fender-play-learn-guitar/id1226057939?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)). Sample is small and partly dated (2018-2025).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Fender itself identifies abandonment (about 90% in year one) as the core problem; design retention in from the start (`ux/onboarding-retention-motivation.md`).
- Backing and isolated tracks are a feature users like; Splitune can generate these for any song via separation.

## Implications for Splitune
Evidence that hardware brands invest in digital learning for retention; their catalogs are narrow, giving room for a "any song" product.

## Open questions
- Official price list and trial terms.
- Whether Fender Play ingests user songs (no evidence).
