# Competitor teardown: Ultimate Guitar

## TL;DR
- Ultimate Guitar (UG, owned by Muse Group) is the largest chords-and-tabs destination: mostly user-generated tabs/chords free, with paid Pro features (interactive "Official" tabs, Simplify, transposition, metronome, autoscroll, tuner, export), plus UG Courses and a Tonebridge effects add-on.
- Pricing per the US App Store listing: Pro $9.99/mo or $29.99-$39.99/yr; "UG Pro + Courses + Sing" bundle $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr; listings are inconsistent.
- 480K App Store ratings at 4.7; Trustpilot only 3.1/5 from 2,004 reviews, dominated by trial-conversion and refund complaints.
- UG has no song-import or transcription from user audio, and no stems. Its moat is catalog and community.
- Confidence is low because UG's own site pages were not fetchable in this pass (only app store data).

## Findings

### Core features
Large catalogue of guitar, bass and ukulele chords, tabs and lyrics with search by genre, rating, difficulty and tuning; tab collections; backing-track videos; playlists, personal tab editing and uploads; left-handed mode, offline access, dark mode; UG Courses; Tonebridge guitar effects. Pro: interactive tabs, chord variations, "Simplify" for difficult songs, transposition, metronome, autoscroll, built-in tuner, export/printing ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/id357828853)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Search → pick a version (ranked by user rating) → read chords/tab with autoscroll → (Pro) interactive tab playback, transposition or "Simplify" → optionally play along with a video as backing track. Quality varies by version.

### Instruments and levels
Guitar, bass, ukulele; courses across instruments and levels ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/id357828853)).

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
US App Store IAPs: Annual Pro Access $29.99; UG Pro+ 1 Year $39.99; UG Pro+ 1 Month $9.99; Ultimate Guitar Pro $39.99 annual / $9.99 monthly; sale entries $22.99/$29.99; and the description lists "UG Pro + UG Courses + UG Sing" at $9.99 monthly / $59.99 yearly, which conflicts with the $39.99 yearly IAP ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/id357828853)). Model: free ad-supported UGC library, paid subscription for Pro/Official content.

### Platforms (official)
iOS 16.1+, iPadOS 16.1+, visionOS ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/id357828853)). A Google Play listing for the Android app appears in search results but could not be opened here; web site not fetched.

### What is public about tech and company
UG is part of Muse Group, whose portfolio includes MuseScore, Audacity, StaffPad and, since December 2023, Hal Leonard; Muse says its platforms have large audiences, relatively few paying users and little licensed material, which Hal Leonard's publisher relationships address ([ScoringNotes](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/)). Muse also cites AI/ML such as StaffPad transcription as a combination opportunity ([ScoringNotes](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/)).

### Standout strengths
Unmatched catalog and community ratings; recognized brand; licensed-publisher path via Hal Leonard; many monetization surfaces (Pro, Courses, Sing, Tonebridge).

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
- Trustpilot 3.1/5 (2,004 reviews; 31% one-star): free trials that convert unexpectedly, refunds refused, cancellation hard to find, generic support; praise for catalog, loop/slow practice tools and short lessons ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ultimate-guitar.com)).
- App Store: glitchy metronome/tuner, outages, ads and upsell prompts; praise for library size and chord diagrams ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/id357828853?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Community-sourced tabs have unpredictable accuracy; machine-made tabs with confidence scores could be more consistent.
- The "Simplify" feature is the only mainstream difficulty adaptation in this category; study it for the roadmap in `product/feature-roadmap-beginner-to-intermediate.md`.
- Avoid dark-pattern trial conversion; it dominates UG's negative sentiment (see `ux/onboarding-retention-motivation.md`).

## Implications for Splitune
UG is a content moat, not a technology moat. Splitune competes by covering songs UG users can't find in good versions and by tailoring to the learner's level and instrument.

## Open questions
- Official site pricing and feature split (free vs Pro vs Official tabs).
- How UG licenses Official tabs (royalty structure).
