# Competitor teardown: Melodics

## TL;DR
- Melodics (Melodics Limited) teaches drums (also keys/pads on other platforms) with 500+ lessons covering rudiments, grooves, fills, drills and songs. The iPhone app listens through the microphone to acoustic drums or a practice pad (calibration required) or connects to e-kits over Bluetooth/wired.
- iPhone listing: first 20 lessons free; subscription $34.99/month or $179.99/year. A brand-new iPhone app (630 ratings, 4.7).
- Reviews (2025) mention a scoring-system overhaul, menu resets, MIDI connection feedback, and feature requests for custom phrases and notation.
- Confidence is low: the official site pricing page returned 404 and only the iPhone App Store listing was opened.
- Splitune takeaway: drum learning is feedback-heavy and gear-flexible; mic-based drum detection works well enough to ship.

## Findings

### Core features
Uses the iPhone's built-in mic to recognize acoustic drums or a pad; electronic kits via Bluetooth or wired; 500+ lessons; slow down and loop; progress tracking; first 20 lessons free ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/melodics-drum-lessons-app/id6504540940)). Developer claim "6x more likely to improve" is marketing, not independently verified.

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Calibrate → pick lesson/grade → play with note lanes and instant per-hit feedback → repeat. Songs are part of the library.

### Instruments and levels
Drums in this listing; other platforms cover keys/pads (search summary; not verified). Grades 1+.

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
Monthly $34.99, annual $179.99; 20 free lessons ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/melodics-drum-lessons-app/id6504540940)). Blog posts quoted other prices; not opened.

### Platforms (official)
iPhone, iOS 16+, iPhone-only listing ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/melodics-drum-lessons-app/id6504540940)); other platforms not verified.

### What is public about tech and company
Mic-based drum recognition after calibration. No further detail.

### Standout strengths
Per-hit feedback; works on pads and acoustic kits with no extra gear.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
New scoring system harder to follow; menus resetting to Grade 1; no MIDI connection feedback; colors; wanting saved custom phrases, stricter precision, auto BPM above lesson tempo, notation ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/melodics-drum-lessons-app/id6504540940?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Drum feedback via mic is credible; see `ml/transcription-drums.md` for transcription side.
- Notation and phrase saving are requested; include them.

## Implications for Splitune
Drums are a differentiated segment with higher price ($180/yr). A drum lesson derived from an actual song's drum stem would be unique.
