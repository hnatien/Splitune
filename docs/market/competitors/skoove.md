# Competitor teardown: Skoove

## TL;DR
- Skoove (Learnfield GmbH) is an AI-feedback piano-lesson app: 500+ lessons and courses in short sections, listening via microphone, USB MIDI or Bluetooth MIDI, tempo control and looping, instructional videos, a song library from pop to classical.
- Premium only in the App Store listing: ten different "Skoove Premium" entries between $4.99 and $149.99 (some with free trial); currency not stated. Official pricing was not readable.
- 4.6/5 from 22K ratings; reviewers cite login problems, misleading trials and microphone issues in noisy rooms.
- Confidence is low: only the App Store listing and its reviews could be opened.

## Findings

### Core features
AI-based real-time feedback; tempo control and looping; instructional videos; theory, note reading, sight-reading and technique; song library including Bach, Beethoven, Debussy, Mozart and pop; USB MIDI, Bluetooth MIDI or acoustic piano by microphone; developer claims 2 million users (self-reported) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skoove-learn-to-play-piano/id1160668178)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Short lesson segments with video → play along → feedback → move on. Curated catalog; no import.

### Instruments and levels
Piano; "Lessons from Beginners to Pro" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skoove-learn-to-play-piano/id1160668178)).

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
IAP entries named "Skoove Premium": $149.99; free-trial entries $89.99, $119.99, $9.99, $59.99, $4.99; plain entries $29.99, $19.99, $59.99, $39.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skoove-learn-to-play-piano/id1160668178)). The listing does not map prices to durations. Search results quoted web prices of $29.99/mo, $19.99/mo for 3 months, $12.49/mo for 12 months; not opened, unverified.

### Platforms (official)
iPhone/iPad/iPod touch, iOS 15.2+ ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skoove-learn-to-play-piano/id1160668178)). Android likely (search results), unverified.

### What is public about tech and company
Learnfield GmbH; "AI that listens"; no technical disclosure.

### Standout strengths
Large lesson count, short segments, MIDI-friendly, clear structure.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
Repeated logouts, trial seen as misleading, few songs free, subscription too costly for some, microphone struggles in noise ("complete silence" needed), pop-up interruptions, lesson pacing too fast for beginners, piano sound quality ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skoove-learn-to-play-piano/id1160668178?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Same lessons as flowkey/Simply: mic robustness and trial transparency (`users/voice-of-customer-pain-points.md`).

## Implications for Splitune
Piano apps converge on mic feedback, which is not a differentiator; transcription of chosen songs could be.
