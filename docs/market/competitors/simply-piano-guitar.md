# Competitor teardown: Simply Piano and Simply Guitar (Simply / JoyTunes)

## TL;DR
- Simply Ltd (formerly JoyTunes) runs the largest beginner-focused listening-feedback apps: Simply Piano (858K App Store ratings, 4.7) and Simply Guitar (399K, 4.7), plus Piano Maestro and Simply Sing. The app listens through the phone/tablet microphone and tells you if you played the right notes.
- Subscription with a free trial and two free courses; US App Store price lists are many and unlabeled, spanning roughly $19.99 to $199.99 for guitar and $24.95 to $179.99 for piano.
- Complaints (anecdotal): subscription cost and aggressive paywalls, trial/billing surprises, note recognition that credits wrong notes or rejects right ones, and difficulty spikes.
- Splitune takeaway: the market for guided beginner apps is large and monetizes at about $100-$200/yr, but their trust problem (billing, feedback accuracy) is an opening for a more transparent product.

## Findings

### Core features
Simply Piano: courses for different tastes and levels, songs (e.g., pop and classical), step-by-step basics from reading sheet music to two hands, a song library that can be slowed down, personalized 5-minute workouts, Touch Courses for on-screen keyboard; place the device on the piano and the app "recognizes what you play" and gives instant feedback ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-piano-learn-piano-fast/id1019442026)). Simply Guitar: step-by-step video lessons, chords, strumming, tabs, built-in tuner, progress tracking, feedback on whether you're playing correctly ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-guitar-learn-guitar/id1476695335)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Install → pick course → watch short lesson → play along; the app highlights notes/chords and gives pass/fail feedback → unlock more songs via premium. Songs come from the curated library; there is no import of arbitrary songs.

### Instruments and levels
Piano, guitar (separate apps), sing, draw; beginner-heavy with advanced courses ([Simply Piano site](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano)).

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
- Simply Piano (US App Store): "12 months Premium with trial" $149.99 / $119.99; Individual Plan $24.95 / $119.99; Family Plan $34.95 / $179.99; 3 months Premium $74.99; 12 months Premium (20% off) $95.99; Full Access $19.99; users play two free courses ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-piano-learn-piano-fast/id1019442026)). The listing does not map prices to plans.
- Simply Guitar: Full Access $19.99, $29.99, $119.99, $179.99; Premium $119.99, $149.99, $199.99; Individual Plan $19.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-guitar-learn-guitar/id1476695335)).
- The official piano page lists no prices ([hellosimply.com](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano)).

### Platforms (official)
iOS/iPadOS 15+ and Apple Vision ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-piano-learn-piano-fast/id1019442026)); App Store and Google Play, Chromebook, Apple Vision Pro, Android XR for piano ([Simply Piano site](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano)).

### What is public about tech and company
Developed by JoyTunes, now Simply Ltd; Editors' Choice; Trinity College London certification badge on piano ([Simply Piano site](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano)). The technology for listening is not described.

### Standout strengths
Beginner-friendly pacing, streak/motivation mechanics, brand awareness, ad-free lessons, instrument-free setup.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
- Simply Piano App Store: subscription cost most common, free-version limits, difficulty spikes, no way to reset lessons, login/sync issues ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-piano-learn-piano-fast/id1019442026?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).
- Simply Guitar: app credits chords not being played, tuning feedback that led one user to break a string, electric guitar without amp not registering, "behind a monthly paywall" ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-guitar-learn-guitar/id1476695335?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).
- Trustpilot (hellosimply.com): 3.0/5 from 9 reviews; cancellation and unexpected charges ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellosimply.com)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Ear-based feedback accuracy is a trust issue across the category; if added, show confidence and allow manual override (`ux/practice-tool-patterns.md`).
- Gating nearly everything after 2 free courses is a top complaint; consider letting the user complete one full real song free.
- Provide popular real songs at the right difficulty rather than arrangements chosen by the vendor.

## Implications for Splitune
Beginner segment is price-tolerant (about $100-$200/yr) but wary of subscription traps. See `market/pricing-and-business-models.md`.
