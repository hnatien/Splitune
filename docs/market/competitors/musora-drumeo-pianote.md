# Competitor teardown: Musora (Drumeo, Pianote, Guitareo, PlayBass, Singeo)

## TL;DR
- Musora Media Inc. unifies its instrument-specific brands (Drumeo drums, Pianote piano, Guitareo guitar, PlayBass bass, Singeo singing) in one app: step-by-step lessons from well-known teachers, 5,000+ professionally transcribed songs with sheet music, drum charts, chord charts and tabs, loop/tempo tools, and "instrument removal from the mix".
- Premium-priced: Drumeo/Pianote monthly $30 or annual $279 ($23.25/mo) with songs; $25/$229 without songs (as shown on the plan pages), 7-day trial, 90-day money-back guarantee. App Store shows Musora monthly $29.99 and annual $279.00.
- No real-time listening feedback is described; value is teacher quality and library breadth, with a community.
- Complaint (anecdotal): a lifetime-member reviewer says access to Songs was restricted when rules changed.
- Splitune takeaway: the high end of the curriculum market (about $280/yr) sells teachers, not tech; songs are transcribed by people.

## Findings

### Core features
Guided learning paths with daily lessons; library of 5,000+ transcribed songs with sheet music, drum charts, chord charts, tabs; loop, tempo and instrument removal; progress tracking and offline practice logging; offline downloads; community of 100,000+ ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277)). Drumeo plan page: 200+ courses, practice workouts, 2,500+ songs with playback tools, unlimited personal support, mentor/live Q&A features ([Drumeo plans](https://www.drumeo.com/choose-plan)). Pianote page: same price structure ([Pianote plans](https://www.pianote.com/choose-plan)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Choose instrument → follow method/courses → Songs section: pick a transcribed song, view charts with playback tools, loop/slow/remove instrument. Songs come from the vendor's team.

### Instruments and levels
Drums, piano, guitar, bass, singing; beginner to advanced ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277)).

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
- Drumeo plan page: Annual $279 ($23.25/mo) with songs; Monthly $30 with songs; a second listing shows $229/yr ($19.08/mo) and $25/mo; lessons-only option 17% cheaper without songs; 7-day free trial; 90-day money-back guarantee ([Drumeo plans](https://www.drumeo.com/choose-plan)). The page has an internal discrepancy: annual saving "$83/year" vs FAQ "roughly $81".
- Pianote plan page: same prices and trial ([Pianote plans](https://www.pianote.com/choose-plan)).
- Musora App Store: Musora monthly $29.99, annual $279.00; Drumeo Edge monthly $29.99, annual $239.99; one-off course purchases $6.99-$29.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277)).

### Platforms (official)
iPhone/iPad iOS 16.4+, Mac with Apple silicon (not verified for macOS), Apple Vision ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277)); web access via drumeo.com and pianote.com.

### What is public about tech and company
Musora Media Inc. is seller and copyright holder; instructors named include Chad Smith, Kenny Aronoff, Jordan Rudess ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277)). No ML claims.

### Standout strengths
Teacher quality and celebrity instructors; breadth across five instruments; strong guarantee; offline downloads; human-transcribed songs.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
Lifetime member felt misled when Songs access changed (Feb 2025); video player glitches and resume feature unreliable (2021); AirPlay limits (2024); praise for value versus in-person lessons ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musora-the-music-lessons-app/id1460388277?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- Learners pay $280/yr for human-curated depth, so the ceiling is high if quality is high.
- Lifetime plans create future-liability and trust issues when licensing changes; avoid or define clearly.

## Implications for Splitune
Splitune could position as "Musora-like lesson quality on any song, automatically", but must be honest about machine-generated quality.

## Open questions
- Current prices vs the two listings; revenue and retention.
