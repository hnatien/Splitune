# Competitor teardown: JustinGuitar app (Musopia)

## TL;DR
- The "Justin Guitar Lessons & Songs" app (developer Musopia, seller Musopia Oy) packages JustinGuitar's beginner Grades 1-3 course: 210 video lessons (36+ hours), a 10-minute Daily Practice Routine, one-minute chord change drills, a color-chord system, and 1,500+ songs with adjustable tempo, lyrics, next-chord diagrams and optional vocals, plus a tuner.
- Free to download; US App Store IAPs from $8.99 (1 month Full Access) to $129.99 (Justin Unlimited Access 1 year), plus a $89.99 "Learn Guitar Pass"; 4.8/5 from about 27K ratings.
- Reviews praise the teaching style; complaints focus on missing support and aggressive pacing in later song videos.
- A newer Musopia app for advanced beginners/intermediates was mentioned in a JustinGuitar community thread, but we could not open it (HTTP 403). Confidence is low.
- Splitune takeaway: a trusted teacher brand with a mostly free web course remains the benchmark for beginner guidance; song-learning apps will be compared with it.

## Findings

### Core features
Structured learning path; interactive exercises; One-Minute Chord Change drills; strumming pattern exercises; song player with adjustable tempo, lyrics, next-chord diagrams or strumming patterns, optional vocals; playlists; songbook with chord filter; tuner; progress tracking ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504)).

### UX flow of the "learn a song" path
Follow Grade 1-3 lessons → play songs that use the chords you have learned in the song player, with chord prompts and tempo control. Songs are curated.

### Instruments and levels
Guitar; beginner (Grades 1-3) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504)).

### Pricing and business model (accessed 2026-10-20)
US App Store IAPs: Full Access 1 Month $8.99; Unlock Guitar Lessons & Songs $9.99; Justin Unlimited Access 1 Month $10.99 and $19.99; Full Access 1 Year $64.99; Learn Guitar Pass $89.99; Justin Unlimited Access 1 Year $99.99 and $129.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504)). Entries repeat and are not labeled by tier.

### Platforms (official)
iOS/iPadOS 15+, Mac with Apple silicon (not verified for macOS), Apple Vision ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504)). The JustinGuitar website could not be fetched (HTTP 403).

### What is public about tech and company
Lessons by Justin Sanderson; the listing claims 4.5M+ students (self-reported); development by Musopia ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504)). No tech disclosures.

### Standout strengths
Trusted teacher, encouraging style, clear progression, play-along songs improving timing and chord changes.

### Common complaints (tier 4, anecdotal)
No direct support contact (one user locked out a month), Bluetooth audio bug after an update, later song videos teach tough sections too quickly ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/justin-guitar-lessons-songs/id1176125504?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).

### What Splitune can learn or do differently
- A one-minute chord-change drill and a daily 10-minute routine are concrete, evidence-friendly practice patterns; see `research/ux/learning-science-principles.md`.
- Tie songs to chords the learner already knows (song selection by known chords) -- a feature Splitune can automate across any song.

## Implications for Splitune
Beginner progress should be judged against a free, well-loved curriculum. Splitune's added value is "your song, at your level".
