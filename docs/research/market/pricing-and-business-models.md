# Competitor pricing and business models

## TL;DR
- Prices cluster in four bands (US list, per month equivalent): practice utilities and chord/tab tools about $2-$10; AI transcription about $3-$25 (tickets or caps); structured-learning subscriptions $8-$25; premium human-taught platforms about $23-$30. One-time purchases persist for desktop tools ($14.99 to $198).
- Free tiers are almost always usage- or content-capped, not time-limited: Chordify daily songs, Songsterr/Klangio 20-second previews, LALAL.AI 10 minutes, Simply two free courses, Melodics 20 lessons, Rocksmith+ 20 songs, Moises limited uploads (cap unpublished).
- Where server inference is the cost (separation, transcription), vendors meter it: minutes (LALAL.AI), tickets (Klangio), monthly caps (Songsterr: 50 tabs). Public price-per-processed-minute anchors sit around $0.05-$0.08 (LALAL.AI top-ups) and about $0.05-$1.00 per transcription (Klangio tickets/tokens).
- Store and web prices often disagree (Songsterr $2.99 web, seen earlier in this session but not visible on the Plus page at re-check and unverified, vs $9.99 App Store; Moises web vs app), and many store listings show unlabeled price ladders. Treat every number here as "listed on 2026-10-20", not stable.
- Splitune implication: a single subscription priced in the $5-$12/month band with a capped number of full-song analyses fits market norms; per-song cost must stay below about $0.28 just to break even at an $8 plan, 30% store fee and 20 songs/month (see Implications), and well below that to keep margin, which favors on-device or low-cost self-hosted analysis. Cross-user shared/cached analysis is not available as a lever without counsel approval.

### Business model types
| Model | Examples | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription, freemium | Moises, Chordify, Songsterr, Chord ai, UG, Yousician, Simply, Fender Play, flowkey, Skoove, Melodics, Musora | Recurring revenue; trial-conversion and renewal practices dominate negative reviews (Trustpilot samples for UG, Yousician, Moises, Simply). |
| Minutes / credits | LALAL.AI | Fast/relaxed queues; one-time top-up packs; subscriptions with monthly allotments. |
| Tickets / tokens | Klangio | Pay per full transcription or monthly ticket allotment; app-store reviewers called it misleading when demos only show seconds. |
| Monthly cap on AI | Songsterr (50 full tabs/month) | Predictable cost per user. |
| One-time purchase | Transcribe!, Anytune, Guitar Pro, RipX | No recurring revenue; desktop buyers accept $15-$200. |
| Perpetual + optional subscription | Capo (Mac retail $99, subscriptions since moving off one-time) | Reviewers noted grandfathering. |
| B2B / API | Music.AI (Moises' business arm), LALAL.AI API, Klangio API, Soundslice Licensing | Music.AI claims over two million minutes processed per day ([music.ai](https://music.ai/)); Soundslice sells embeds at $100/mo per 200 unique users. |
| Licensed catalog | Rocksmith+, Fender Songs (closed), Songsterr (royalties), Chordify (publisher partnerships), flowkey Classic vs Premium tiers | Content licensing is the biggest fixed cost for catalog products. |
| Hardware tie-in | Fender Play / Songs (10% off gear for Songs subscribers) | Retention aim: Fender cited a ~90% first-year abandonment rate for guitar lessons ([Marketing Dive](https://marketingdive.com/news/guitar-maker-fender-strikes-chord-with-new-songs-subscription-app/565629)). |
| Human-taught premium | Musora family | $279/yr; 90-day guarantee. |

### Licensing as a cost line
- Songsterr states it pays "almost half" of revenue as royalties (self-reported) and removes tabs where a publisher has not licensed or has restricted content ([Songsterr help](https://www.songsterr.com/help)).
- Rocksmith+ had to renegotiate all music and lost most Rocksmith 2014 songs at launch; a smaller team now relicenses removed tracks and uses UGC for licensed songs ([dev diary](https://www.ubisoft.com/pl-pl/game/rocksmith/plus/news-updates/1R73uTJE3SninROom780EG/rocksmith-dev-diary-july-2021), [dev update](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/game/rocksmith/plus/news-updates/6QLRU2tVz1xa2X2qFNS9b/rocksmith-dev-team-update-where-we-are-whats-next-and-where-we-stand)).
- flowkey splits tiers by content class: Classic (classical and non-copyrighted songs) vs Premium (full library) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flowkey-learn-to-play-piano/id1020357408)).
- Muse Group bought Hal Leonard (Dec 2023) citing the gap between large audiences with few paying users and little licensed content ([ScoringNotes](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/)).
- Chordify says it negotiated partnerships with music publishers ([Techleap](https://techleap.nl/blog/chordify-striking-the-right-note-in-music-education-and-ai-innovation)).

### Server-cost anchors visible in public prices (inference, not vendor cost)
- LALAL.AI sells separation at about $0.060-$0.067 per processed minute via top-ups ($50/750, $190/3,000, $300/5,000 min) and about $0.06 per fast minute on Pro ($15 per 250 min, annual-equivalent) ([pricing](https://www.lalal.ai/pricing/)). The Lite plan ($7.50/mo for 90 Fast Queue minutes, about $0.083/min) is pricier per minute. Paid plans include unlimited Relaxed Queue minutes, so these per-minute figures apply to Fast Queue minutes only. Minutes are multiplied by the number of stem types selected, so a 4-minute song with 4 stems bills as 16 minutes (about $1 at top-up rates).
- Klangio: single token $2.99; pack of 50 at $24.99 (about $0.50 each); Universe at $149.99/yr with 250 tickets/month is about $0.05 per ticket if fully used ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/piano2notes/id1589420929), [help](https://klang.io/help/klangio-subscriptions/)).
- Songsterr: $2.99/mo (web price seen earlier in this session; not visible on the Plus page at re-check; unverified) with a 50-complete-tabs-per-month cap ([Plus](https://www.songsterr.com/plus)) implies at most about $0.06 revenue per tab if the cap is fully used (or about $0.20 at the $9.99 App Store price). That either means very cheap inference, low utilization of the cap, or both; unverified.

## Implications for Splitune
- Unit economics sketch (illustrative arithmetic, not evidence): monthly margin = price x (1 - store fee) - songs per month x cost per song. With a $8 plan, a 30% store fee (placeholder; see `research/legal/youtube-copyright-and-platform-policy.md` for store policy) and 20 songs per month, cost per song must stay below about $0.28 to break even before support, licensing and infrastructure overhead. If cost per song is near LALAL.AI's retail $0.06/min times a 4-minute song and several stems, GPU inference quickly consumes the margin; get measured cost per song-minute from `research/ml/deployment-and-inference.md`.
- Strategies seen in the market to control cost: local/on-device processing (Capo, RipX), monthly caps (Songsterr), tickets (Klangio), minute allotments (LALAL.AI), free previews (Songsterr/Klangio 20 s).
- Cross-user result sharing (blocked pending legal review, not counted in the unit economics above): reusing one user's analysis of a recording for other users would cut inference cost, but a shared cache raises distribution exposure; results stay private per user until counsel approves (see `research/legal/youtube-copyright-and-platform-policy.md`).
- Pricing position: the whitespace is between "$3-$10 tools with no curriculum" and "$100-$280/yr curricula on curated songs". A tier around $8-$12/month (or $60-$100/yr) with a clear monthly cap of full-song analyses would sit where Chord ai ($69.99/yr), Klangio Pro ($99.99/yr) and Fender Play low-end plans sit.
- Avoid: surprise trial conversions and hidden caps (top negative themes), lifetime plans tied to licensed content, and tickets that show only a few seconds in the free preview without explanation.
