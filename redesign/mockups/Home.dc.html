How a session goes

  1. 1

    Bring a song

    Upload a file, paste a link or record it. It plays at once.

  2. 2

    Pick your part

    We isolate it and work out the beat, chords and tab in a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Loop the hard bars

    Slow them down, add a count-in, and rate each pass.

  4. 4

    Speed up as it gets clean

    Come back tomorrow and see what stuck.
