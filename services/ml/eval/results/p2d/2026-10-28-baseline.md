# Prototype 2d — drums (2026-10-28, baseline)

git `21ff0b8+dirty` · ADTOF `adtofAll_0-pt-85c192e` · YourMT3+ `mc13_256_g4_all_v7_mt3f_…` · separator per chain · device `cuda` (this PC: RTX 3060) · items built: {'real-mdb': 8, 'real-enst': 8, 'solo-drums': 4, 'rendered-band': 18, 'drum-machine': 4}.

Metrics: onset F1 / P / R per item, summed over the item's classes (mir_eval matching per class at ±50 ms, and ±30 ms), then the item mean per group; class-mean F1 averages the classes present in the reference. 3-class tiers (`real-electronic`, `solo-drums`) score kick, snare and hi-hat only. Bars: both sides through the shipped `quantizeDrumHits` (music-core CLI `quantize-drums`) on the beats stage's grid of the mixture; `bar exact` = share of bars whose (class, beat, tick) set matches the reference's exactly, `bar Jaccard` = mean per-bar overlap. Kit pieces: among class-matched hits, share whose kit piece matches (hi-hat open vs closed, crash vs ride, tom line). Time: wall seconds per 30 s of audio for notes.drums (and separation for stem inputs), cold model loads included once per run.

In-sample notes: YourMT3+ trained on ENST-Drums and Slakh (its `real-enst` row is in-sample); MDB, ENST and RBMA13 are test sets in ADTOF's paper. uSDR is scale-sensitive and MDB's drum-only track is not at the full mix's gain, so `real-mdb` uSDR is not a separation score. YourMT3+'s `stem` condition equals `chosen` and is not run twice.
