# Prototype 1: separation inference settings (2026-10-21)

Why: Step 07 measured RoFormers at up to 575 s for a 244.5 s song (viperx), near the 600 s
per-model budget (06 §2) and far over "full analysis ≤ 3 min" (25 §5). The fix proposed was a
per-model inference setting in `config/separation.personal.yaml`, chosen on the P1 clips.

## What `overlap` means in audio-separator 0.44.5
- **MDXC / RoFormer** (`mdxc_separator.py`, `demix`, `is_roformer` branch): `overlap` is the
  **window step in seconds**, `step = min(overlap × 44100, chunk)`, with
  `chunk = stft_hop_length × (inference.dim_t − 1)`. It is not a divisor: a smaller value means
  more windows. The default 8 is already close to the cheapest:
  | Model | Chunk | Windows per 244.5 s at default (step 8 s) | Cheapest step |
  |---|---:|---:|---|
  | bs_roformer_sw | 9.29 s (512 × 800) | 31 | 9.29 s → 27 windows (−13%) |
  | bs_roformer_viperx_1297 | 8.00 s (441 × 800) | 31 (no overlap already) | = default |
  | mel_roformer_kim_vocals | 11.0 s (441 × 1100) | 31 | 11.0 s → 23 windows (−26%) |
  `overlap` 4 and 2 double and quadruple the work. (The non-RoFormer MDXC branch does divide:
  `hop = chunk // overlap`; none of the map's models use it.)
- **Demucs**: `shifts` = random-shift passes averaged (default 2; time ∝ shifts), `overlap` =
  segment fraction (0.25).
- One 8 s viperx window takes 7.7 s on the M4 GPU (MPS, fp32), all of it model compute
  (cProfile: 99% waiting on the GPU). The Step 07 575 s did not reproduce: 269 s and 313 s
  today at the same settings, i.e. run-to-run variance on this Mac is about ±15% and the Step 07
  figure was inflated (most likely other load on the machine).

(`splitune_eval.scoring.usdr`) against MUSDB references for vocals, drums and bass on every clip

**Chosen (bold)**: the cheapest setting that loses < 0.3 dB median uSDR against the default on
every referenced instrument. Guitar and piano (no references) use the same model's setting.
- htdemucs_6s, htdemucs_ft: `shifts: 1` (≈ 0.55× time, worst −0.11 dB).
- mel_roformer_kim_vocals: `overlap: 11` (step = chunk; 0.77× time on clips, −0.09 dB).
- bs_roformer_sw: `overlap: 9.3` (step = chunk; worst −0.10 dB; saving expected on songs only).
- bs_roformer_viperx_1297: default (its chunk is 8 s, so no cheaper step exists).
- More overlap buys ≤ +0.14 dB for 2× time; not worth it under the budgets.

## Budgets
- **600 s per model in the chain (06 §2): all fit**; slowest viperx 313–319 s.
- **Stage timeout 1500 s (StageSpec)**: a chain head that fails at the very end plus its
  fallback, cold: vocals Kim 149 + viperx 319 = 468 s (whole four-model vocals chain 739 s);
  bass/drums SW 177 + ft 94 + 6s 36 = 307 s; guitar/piano SW 177 + 6s 36 = 213 s. **All fit.**
- **Full analysis ≤ 3 min for a 4-min song (25 §5): not met** for any instrument whose chain
  head is BS-Roformer-SW (separation alone ≈ 180 s, before decode, tag, stem_post, notes,
  chords). Vocals (Kim, 136 s) leave ~45 s for the rest. Inference settings cannot close this
  gap; options are an owner decision (see PROGRESS.md, 2026-10-21 Step 07b).
