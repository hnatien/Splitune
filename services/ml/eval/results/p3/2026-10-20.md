## Reading
- **Span compliance: pass** (100%, by construction: candidates above maxSpan are never generated;
  42 notes in chords no 4-fret hand can hold are reported `unplayable` instead).
- **Agreement: fail** (0.789 vs 0.90). GuitarSet soloists play almost entirely in position (2.3%
  open strings, median fret 7, 96% of consecutive fretted notes within 3 frets) and choose among
  several equally playable positions for the same notes; the cost model cannot tell which one a
  given player picked. Comping is closer (0.846) but jazz/bossa voicings outside the shape library
  sit in positions 3–8 that the costs do not prefer.
- `easiest` deliberately disagrees with the players (open-position beginner bias, D3), so its
  0.596 is not a defect; the gate is evaluated on `as_played`.
- The ground truth itself exceeds a 4-fret span in 110 onset groups, so 100% agreement is
  impossible with maxSpan 4.

## Proposed next steps (gate not lowered)
1. **Audio string cues:** add a per-note string-likelihood term from the audio (TabCNN-style string
   posteriors on the guitar stem; timbre differs between the same pitch on different strings).
   This is the information the symbolic DP lacks; it fits as one more local cost term.
2. **Learned assigner** (doc 08 fail path): a small sequence model trained on GuitarSet
   (leave-one-player-out) plus synthetic tabs, with the DP kept as the hard playability filter.
3. **Larger voicing library** (jazz shells, drop-2/drop-3, 9ths/13ths) for the comp shape prior.
4. Report the Basic Pitch → DP number (doc 07, no gate) once step 08 installs basic-pitch and
   GuitarSet audio is downloaded.
5. Product fallback per doc 08 until then: rhythm parts default to chord diagrams; tab keeps the
   `easiest` fingering and offers the alternatives (`alt_fingerings`) on tap.
