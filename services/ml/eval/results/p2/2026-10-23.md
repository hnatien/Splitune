YourMT3+ and Transkun were trained on MAESTRO's training split: the piano excerpts come from its test split (unseen performances, but the same Disklavier pianos and rooms), so MAESTRO scores are optimistic for pop piano; BabySlakh's piano class includes electric pianos, clavinet and harpsichord (General MIDI programs 0-7).
