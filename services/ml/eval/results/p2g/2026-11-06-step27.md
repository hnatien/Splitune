# Step 27: guitar accuracy follow-up (2026-11-06)

Goal: raise guitar accuracy end to end on P2g and keep a change only if it measurably beats the
shipped chain on held-out items. End-to-end report: `2026-11-06-final.md`
(`p2g.py report --variant shipped s27 --baseline shipped --name final`); per-candidate reports
alongside it.

## Test set and method

- 128 items (was 57). Three held-out tiers on hard-band stems: GuitarSet player 05 (33 items),
  Guitar-TECHS (24: player 2 and the P3 music pieces outside the fit split), IDMT-SMT-Guitar
  (20: the FS and LP guitars, dataset 2 licks and short pieces). The fit split (`is_fit_id`) is
  GuitarSet players 03-04, Guitar-TECHS player 1 drills and every third music piece, and IDMT's
  AR guitar. GuitarSet players 00-03 never count as test (in Guitab's training).
- GOAT is only available on request (Zenodo 15690894), and EGDB's Google Drive mirror
  rate-limited this machine after 57 DI files, so neither is in the set.
- Every table pairs items: 95 % intervals from 2000 item bootstrap resamples (seed 27, the
  `splitune_eval.bootstrap` module). Onset metrics are item means, Tab F1 and TDR pooled counts;
  Δ is the paired difference to the baseline and ✓ marks an interval that excludes 0.
- The baseline is the shipped chain re-run today (`shipped`): BS-Roformer-SW → Guitab E25 + A4
  compensation + bends → `buildTab`, string cue 2.

## Vibrato items at onset F1 0 (finding 5)

The fault is in the separator; the reference and the matching are fine. On `techs-P1-vibrato`
and `techs-P2-vibrato`, Guitab finds 9/10 and 11/11 notes on the clean guitar. The audio/MIDI
lag is ≤ 30 ms. BS-Roformer-SW puts these sustained low-string single notes (F2-D3) into the
**bass** stem: the guitar stem sits at −99.6 / −99.3 dB on the easy band, and Guitab finds
9/10 and 11/11 notes on the bass stem. The vibrato windows on higher strings separate normally
(6-8 of 11 hits). htdemucs_6s and becruily's guitar model did the same in step 22. The same
happens to low palm-muted riffs (`techs-P1-palmmute`, −110 dB on the hard band). Nothing in
the transcription can fix it, and none of the separators tried keeps them (below).

## Shipped: `s27`

`notes.guitar` params on top of the old chain (`config/notes.personal.yaml`):

- `extend_threshold: 0.2` (`guitab.extend_offsets`): a note's end moves on while its string's
  activity stays above 0.2, up to the next note on that string. Starts and frets stay as they
  were, so onsets and Tab F1 do not move. The threshold was chosen on fit (fit mean onset+offset
  F1 0.333 → 0.496; 0.1/0.3/0.45 lower).
- `bend_min_glide_frames: 2` (from 3): chosen on fit among 50/60/75 cents × 2/3 frames.
  Held-out bend hits 30 → 34 of 52 at precision 0.938 → 0.872 (bend F1 0.714 → 0.747). On all
  items: 50 → 56 of 78, precision 0.926 → 0.889.
- `downtune: true` (`stage.down_tuning`, `Guitab.tuning_shift`): Guitab runs again on the stem
  raised by 1 and 2 semitones. A guitar tuned k semitones down rings its open strings only when
  raised by k. If one shift has a clear open-string share (≥ 0.08, ≥ 0.05 above the unshifted
  share and ≥ 10× it), the notes are transcribed at that shift, mapped back, and the tuning
  suggestion names half/full step down. Otherwise the step-22 low-note cue applies (sustained
  confident notes below E2). The rule was set on fit (0 false positives; looser rules gave 1-2
  false positives on test). The check reads at most 60 s of a recording (start, middle and end
  pieces: `downtune_max_s`), so its cost is fixed per song.
