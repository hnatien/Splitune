## ft2: the fine-tune

`eval/p2p_finetune.py`: 297 training excerpts (+ 32 held out for checkpoint choice) — 240 POP909
songs no P2p item uses, rendered through every p2p renderer (Salamander, Splendid, Headroom, upright,
jRhodes, Wurlitzer, CP80, harpsichord, GM clavinet) with room / phone / detune augmentation and bar
pedalling on slow songs, half mixed under MUSDB18-HQ drums + bass + vocals (songs no legacy item uses;
`other` left out after `ft1`: its keys bled into the stem unlabelled and taught the model to drop
notes) and separated by BS-Roformer-SW; 48 MAESTRO v3 `train` excerpts (half under a band, separated);
P2p's fit split. From the shipped `2.0.pt`, AdamW 1e-5, batch 4 × 16 s, 2500 steps, 1.6 h; held-out
loss 1555 → 635 (best at the last step).

Detail runs, held-out items:

| input | real-solo | real-band | pop-solo | pop-band | legacy | on+off key | on+off pedal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stem, shipped | 0.942 | 0.969 | 0.737 | 0.897 | 0.813 | 0.593 | 0.521 |
| stem, ft2 | 0.956 | 0.964 | 0.804 | 0.892 | 0.789 | 0.638 | 0.593 |
| clean, shipped | 0.942 | 0.971 | 0.858 | 0.949 | 0.962 | 0.719 | 0.644 |
| clean, ft2 | 0.956 | 0.963 | 0.935 | 0.947 | 0.948 | 0.763 | 0.714 |

As the only transcriber it fails the acceptance rule (legacy stems −0.024). Production feeds Transkun
the mix itself only when the solo check skips separation, and there it wins, so it ships as the
`solo` entry of notes.piano; stems keep the shipped checkpoint.

phone / room / keys); no tier drops onset F1 (real-solo +0.014, pop-solo +0.077, band tiers 0);

YourMT3+), cell 4 (hands, still PM2S), electric pianos in a band, and velocity on real Disklavier
solo (on+off+vel 0.513 → 0.484).
