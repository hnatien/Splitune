## Phase 6 — pedal, electric pianos, hands, quantization

- **Pedal (gap 6):** the shipped checkpoint already gives key-release offsets: on real Disklavier pianos onset+offset F1 is 0.62 against key-release references and 0.38 against pedal-extended ones (baseline, real-solo). Durations stay key releases; the sustain pedal is kept (`Notes.sustain`) and drawn as pedal marks under the bass staff (alphaTex `\spd`/`\spu`). The roll keeps played length.
- **Electric pianos and keys (gap 9):** rendered Rhodes, Wurlitzer and CP80 transcribe at 0.93–0.98, so the P2 "electric piano 0.68" is Slakh's patches. Harpsichord and clavinet were the weak family through separation (0.62 on stems); the bypass lifts them to 0.94. Per-song mean calibrated confidence correlates 0.82 with per-song onset F1; the existing low-share rule (> 50 % low notes) never fires (worst song 19 %). Owner's choice (2026-10-25, stop point 4): `views.yaml` `min_confidence_mean: {piano: 0.80}` → chords_only (`low_confidence`): 4 of 81 songs, mean onset F1 0.516, none ≥ 0.9.
- **Hands (gap 9):** see the final report's hands table — PM2S 0.927 vs splitHands 0.885 on the 21 ASAP pieces outside PM2S's training split (0.912 vs 0.881 on all 231; ASAP staves, not physical hands). Shipped as `hands: pm2s` in notes.piano.
- **Quantization:** off-grid share (notes > 60 ms from the grid) on the chosen input: real pianos 0.14–0.16 (rubato classical performances on a beat tracker's grid), rendered pop 0.02–0.04 — comparable to guitar's synthetic material; `quantize/` untouched.
