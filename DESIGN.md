# Design System: Splitune

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Page"**

Splitune speaks in a familiar, plain interface language: a black or white ground, greys between them, one weight step between reading and leading, and pills for anything you press. The chrome is deliberately the quietest thing on screen so that the song, the waveform and the notation are the only loud things. It refuses the music-app default of a dark warm stage lit by one glowing accent; there is no brand hue at all.

The interface carries emphasis with the ink itself. The primary action is a pill of the foreground colour (white on black, black on white), and "now" in the music (playhead, loop edges, current beat) is drawn in the same ink. Colour is reserved for meaning: unsure blue, success green, destructive red, six stem colours and two piano hands. The one place colour appears without meaning is Home's abstract art, which sits beside the words that carry each point.

Density changes with the job. Browsing (library, settings, add song, marketing) is generous and calm, separated by whitespace and hairlines; practice is large, bottom-weighted and readable from a music stand, with 64 px main controls and reading sizes chosen by viewing distance.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Pure black (Dark, default) or pure white (Light) ground, achromatic greys, no brand hue.
- The ink is the accent: primary pill, playhead, loop edges, current beat and focus ring.
- Colour only for status, stems and hands, never alone (always paired with text, shape or icon).
- Figtree at 400 and 500; 600 only for the wordmark, the smallest micro labels and practice reading tokens.
- Pills for every control; 12 px fields and cards; 8 px media.
- Hairlines and whitespace instead of boxes; bars with no rule under them.
- Three appearances (Dark, Light, Stage) plus increased contrast and forced colours.

## Colors

A strictly achromatic interface: black, white and the greys between, with chromatic colour held back for status, stems and hands. All values are OKLCH, defined as primitives in `packages/ui-tokens/src/primitives.css` and mapped to semantic roles per appearance in `themes.css`; components use only the semantic roles.

### Primary
- **White Ink / Near-Black Ink** (`white` on Dark, `light-foreground` on Light): the foreground and the primary role at once. It fills the primary pill, the brand disc, the focus ring (`--ring`), the playhead, loop edges, current beat and the selected-tab rule (`--signal`). Hover on the primary pill steps toward the ground (`dark-primary-hover` / `light-primary-hover`).
- **Ink Wash and Ink Line** (`white-soft` + `white-line` on Dark, `ink-soft` + `ink-line` on Light): the "on" state. A selected or toggled-on surface is the ink at low opacity with a half-opacity edge, never a hue.

### Secondary
- **Unsure Blue** (`unsure-dark` / `unsure-light`, each with its surface): low-confidence notes, the hatch over shaky bars, the unsure badge and the Fix queue. The only status colour that appears in notation.
- **Success Green** (`success-dark` / `success-light`, with surfaces): passed, saved, edited.
- **Destructive Red** (`destructive-dark` / `destructive-light`, with surfaces): errors and destructive actions. Warning and info are deliberately neutral (muted text with their icon).

### Tertiary
- **Stem colours** (guitar teal, bass violet, drums sky, vocals pink, keys lime, other stone; a dark and a light value each): stem rows, stem tiles, fretboard and melody marks, charts. They pass a colour-vision-deficiency separation test in both themes and both contrast levels.
- **Hands** (`hand-left` blue, `hand-right` green, same in both themes, with near-black text): the piano roll's two hands.

### Neutral
- **Black Ground** (`black`): Dark's page, the field well and the app bar. **White Ground** (`white`): Light's page, cards and popovers.
- **Raised Grey** (`dark-card`) and **Popover Grey** (`dark-popover`): Dark's cards and floating surfaces. Light keeps them white and separates with hairlines and elevation.
- **Quiet Fill** (`dark-muted` / `light-muted`): secondary pills, the segmented track, badges, emphasis panels, media placeholders. **Hover Fill** (`dark-accent` / `light-accent`): hover on quiet fills and the segmented capsule in Dark.
- **Hairline** (`dark-border` / `light-border`): card edges, list rules, the footer rule. **Strong Hairline** (`dark-border-strong` / `light-border-strong`): the inset ring of outline buttons, chips, outline badges and keycaps. **Field Edge** (`dark-input` / `light-input`): the one boundary of a text field or slot.
- **Muted Text** (`dark-muted-foreground` / `light-muted-foreground`): supporting copy. **Faint Text** (`dark-faint-foreground` / `light-faint-foreground`): placeholders, step numbers, disabled labels.

### Named Rules
**The No Hue Rule.** The interface has no brand colour. Everything that is not a status, a stem, a hand or Home's art is black, white or an achromatic grey. No amber, anywhere.

**The Ink Is Now Rule.** The primary action, the focus ring and every "now" mark in the music use the foreground ink. Nothing else may claim that emphasis.

**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Every colour carries a second cue: badges are words, confidence uses shape and hatch, selection uses a capsule or a rule. Increased contrast and forced colours must still read.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Figtree (metric-matched "Figtree Fallback" over Arial, then system UI)
**Body Font:** Figtree
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (metric-matched fallback over Courier New); Atkinson Hyperlegible Next as an optional reading face

**Character:** One friendly geometric sans does everything, with weight doing the work of hierarchy: 400 for text, 500 for anything that leads. Tracking tightens as sizes grow (-0.01em at body, -0.03em at headline and display).

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, clamp 40 to 64 px, 1.1, -0.03em): Home's centred headline only, plain text that never animates in.
- **Headline** (500, 32 px / 38, -0.02em; 44 px / 48 at `headline-lg`): marketing section leads and closing panels.
- **Title** (500, 22 px / 28, -0.01em): section headers (`h2` on the left, optional text link on the right). **Title small** (500, 17 px / 24) for card and list item titles.
- **Lead** (400, 17 px, 1.65, -0.01em): the one sentence under a headline; capped near 58ch.
- **Body** (400, 15 px / 22, -0.01em): running text; reading width 72ch. **Body small** (400, 13 px / 18) for secondary notes.
- **Label** (500, 14 px / 20): buttons, segmented items, nav; large buttons use 16 px / 22. **Caption** (500, 12 px / 16) for badges; **Micro** (600, 11 px / 14) for the smallest tags.
- **Reading scale** (practice only): tab digits, chord names, the current chord (7 rem at Desk), bar numbers and readouts, sized by distance preset (Desk, Music stand, Floor) times the appearance's read scale (Stage 1.25) times the learner's calibration, with floors (tab digits never below 18 px).

### Named Rules
**The Two Weights Rule.** Figtree runs at 400 and 500. 600 is reserved for the wordmark, micro labels and the practice reading tokens; there is no bold UI copy.

**The Mono Is Literal Rule.** JetBrains Mono appears only where characters must be literal: keycaps, ids and tab digits. Every other number, including interface counters, prices and step numbers, is Figtree with tabular figures.

## Layout

A 4 px grid (`--spacing: 0.25rem`). Page gutters are 16 / 24 / 32 px at phone / `sm` / `lg`, over Tailwind's default breakpoints. Content widths are named containers: library 1260, settings 1180, pricing 1040, marketing 1240, onboarding 1120, forms 400 and reading 72ch.

Browse surfaces are generous and calm. Home separates sections by whitespace alone (96 px, 120 from `sm`), never by alternating bands or rules; short hero copy is centred, everything that is read is left-aligned; lists come before card grids, and a settings group is a heading plus hairline-divided rows. Bars are 64 px (56 on phones, 60 in practice) with no rule under them, sticky on the ground colour; the phone bottom navigation carries a hairline above it.

Density is a shell attribute (`data-density`): browse controls are 44 px, practice secondary controls 48 px and main practice controls 64 px (88 px tall in Stand view), clustered at the bottom edge. A target is the element's own border box; a smaller painted face sits inside a transparent border, so nothing under 44 px is interactive except the 24 px song-map chips on fine pointers.

### Named Rules
**The Whitespace Divides Rule.** Space separates sections; a 1 px hairline separates rows. Boxes are for things you act on, not for grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Cards are bordered, not lifted (`--elevation-1` is a transparent zero shadow). Depth appears only where a surface floats above the page: menus, popovers, tooltips and toasts take `--elevation-2`, dialogs and sheets `--elevation-3`, and the segmented capsule a 1 px hairline shadow. Dark's shadows are heavier than Light's because they must read against black. Under increased contrast and forced colours every component keeps its 1 px border, because a shadow is not a boundary.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float** (Dark `0 2px 6px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 16px 40px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.5)`; Light `0 2px 6px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.04), 0 12px 32px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.1)`): menus, popovers, tooltips, toasts.
- **Modal** (Dark `0 2px 6px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 24px 64px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.6)`; Light `0 2px 6px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.05), 0 24px 64px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.14)`): dialogs and sheets, over a scrim.
- **Capsule** (`0 1px 2px oklch(0 0 0 / 0.12)`): the pressed segment's raised pill.
- **Glow** (`0 0 0 1px` ink line): a 1 px ink ring on selected cards; dropped under increased contrast.

### Named Rules
**The Bordered Not Lifted Rule.** Resting surfaces never cast shadows. Only floating layers do.

## Shapes

One 10 px base radius with steps around it, and a firm split by role: every control is a pill; fields and cards are 12 px; media and thumbnails are 8 px; menu items and tooltips 10 px; dropdown popups and toasts 14 px; popovers 16 px; dialogs and drawers 22 px; phone sheets 24 px top corners; keycaps, tab-digit boxes and checkboxes 4 px. Nothing but a pill is rounder than 24 px.

Borders are 1 px hairlines; outline-style controls draw their edge as an inset ring so the painted face, not the padded target, defines the shape. Drop zones use a dashed strong hairline; the selected tab and loop edges use a 2 px ink rule. Focus is a 2 px ink outline offset 2 px from the painted face (3 px wide under increased contrast; fields offset 1 px).

The brand mark is an ink disc (28 px, radius 13 in a 28 box) crossed by a 2.4 px wave in the ground colour, beside "splitune" in lowercase Figtree 600 at 17 px, -0.02em. The mono tone draws the disc's outline and wave in the current text colour (the footer).

### Named Rules
**The Pill Means Press Rule.** If it is pressable (button, chip, badge, switch, segmented control, slider, beat dot), it is a pill. If it holds content or input, it is 12 px.

## Components

### Buttons
Calm, familiar pills that grow to their target with a transparent border.
- **Shape:** full pill (`rounded.full`); faces 32 / 40 / 48 / 56 px (`sm` / `md` / `lg` / `xl`), each inside a target of at least 44 px (48 in practice density); practice sizes paint a 48 or 64 px face.
- **Primary:** the ink (white on black, black on white), 14 px / 500 label, 18 px horizontal padding at `md`; one per view.
- **Hover / Focus / Press:** hover steps the fill one grey toward the ground; focus is the 2 px ink outline 2 px off the face; press scales to 0.97 over 200 ms on the standard ease.
- **Secondary:** quiet fill with no edge (the default variant). **Outline / Quiet:** transparent with a strong-hairline inset ring. **Ghost:** muted text, fill on hover (bar links). **On:** ink wash with an ink-line ring. **Destructive:** red text and ring, red surface on hover; **destructive solid** for confirmed deletes. **Link:** ink text, underlined.
- **Disabled:** quiet fill, faint text and a field-edge ring, never an opacity fade. **Loading:** keeps its width and shows a spinner after 300 ms.

### Chips and Badges
- **Badges:** 24 px pills in 12 px / 500, always words. Neutral (quiet fill), outline, primary (ink wash), and status variants that pair the status colour as text on its own surface. Warning and info stay neutral with their icon.
- **Chips** (chord chips, section chips, the legend chip): pills with a strong-hairline edge or a dashed edge for the "what does this mean" legend; hover fills quiet.

### Segmented Controls
A borderless quiet-fill pill track 40 px tall with 3 px padding; items are 34 px pill faces inside full-size targets. The pressed item is a raised capsule (Dark: hover fill; Light: white) with foreground text, and one capsule element slides between items on the snappy spring over 400 ms. Under increased contrast the track gains a strong-hairline border.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12 px.
- **Background:** Raised Grey on Dark, white on Light; `emphasis` is a borderless quiet-fill panel (Home's closing panel).
- **Shadow Strategy:** none; see Bordered Not Lifted.
- **Border:** 1 px hairline; footer separated by a hairline rule.
- **Internal Padding:** 16 px, 16 px gap.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 44 px tall (48 in practice density), 12 px corners, the ground colour as a well, a 1 px field-edge border, 16 px horizontal padding, 15 px body text; placeholders in faint text.
- **Focus:** the border turns to the ink on hover and focus, plus the 2 px ink outline at 1 px offset.
- **Error / Disabled:** invalid fields take a destructive border with the message (icon and words) below; disabled fields take the quiet fill and faint text.

### Navigation
- **Bars:** 64 px on the ground colour, sticky, with no rule beneath. Marketing: wordmark left; How it works and Pricing as ghost-pill text links in foreground; Sign in as a secondary pill; "Try an example song" as the one primary pill. On phones everything but the wordmark folds into a Menu button.
- **App bar:** wordmark, then a quiet pill for search and the account menu; each page keeps its own single primary below it. Phones get a 56 px bar and a bottom navigation with a hairline above, active item in foreground, others faint.
- **Footer:** a hairline rule, the mono-tone wordmark in muted text, then plain text links in foreground that fade to muted on hover.

### Keycaps
JetBrains Mono 12 px, 22 px tall, quiet fill, 1 px strong-hairline edge, 4 px corners. Tooltips and the shortcuts list only.

### Transport and Practice Controls
The practice dock holds 64 px pill and circle controls (Play/Pause 72 on phones) with 24 px icons at 1.8 px stroke; filled glyphs only for Play and Pause. The playhead, loop edges and beat-now dot are the ink; the current bar and loop fill are a single grey step off the ground. Every practice action is keyboard and pedal operable and confirms its pressed state within 100 ms.

### Media (Home art)
Colour art on an 8 px corner with a quiet-fill placeholder: one 16:9 lead, two squares (4:3 on phones) and five thumbnails (96 px, 116 from `sm`), each beside the words that carry its point and decorative (`alt=""`). Each piece is a macro still life of the one object its feature is about (a string, a footswitch, a music stand), mostly dissolved into gel-lit colour with one sharp detail and a fine grain. Palette teal, violet, sky, magenta-pink and lime with white light on black, dark grey or steel; no orange, amber, yellow, red, wood or brass; no people, hands, text, screens or interface. Prompts and provenance live in `docs/home-art.md`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use semantic roles from `@splitune/ui-tokens` only (`bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-input`); the default Tailwind palette is cleared on purpose.
- **Do** give each view exactly one ink (primary) pill; everything else is secondary, outline or ghost.
- **Do** draw every "now" mark (playhead, loop edges, current beat, selected tab rule) in the ink.
- **Do** pair every status or confidence colour with words, shape or hatch, and check both themes and increased contrast.
- **Do** keep interactive targets at 44 px (48 secondary practice, 64 main practice) with the painted face inside a transparent border.
- **Do** use Figtree with tabular figures for interface numbers; keep JetBrains Mono for keycaps, ids and tab digits.
- **Do** separate sections with whitespace and rows with a 1 px hairline.
- **Do** use the motion tokens: 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 ms, standard ease by default, and zero durations under reduced motion; the playhead never eases.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a brand hue or any amber, orange or yellow accent; the interface is black, white and grey.
- **Don't** use colour as decoration outside Home's art.
- **Don't** put a rule under a top bar or box a group of settings in a card per setting.
- **Don't** round a control less than a pill, or a field or card other than 12 px.
- **Don't** lift resting cards with shadows; only floating layers get elevation.
- **Don't** fade a disabled control with opacity; use the quiet fill, faint text and a field-edge ring.
- **Don't** use weights above 500 for interface copy, or uppercase tracked labels above headings.
- **Don't** add decorative motion: no parallax, looping decoration, celebration or full-screen beat flashes.
